METHOD NOTE

How it works

The browser's Web Audio API samples your microphone and applies a Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) to each short window. The app finds the strongest frequency, measures energy across the spectrum, and compares a normalized score with your sensitivity setting.

Use as a signal, not a diagnosis. Room noise, microphone hardware, and changing machine load affect readings. This static app processes audio locally and never sends recordings to a server.
